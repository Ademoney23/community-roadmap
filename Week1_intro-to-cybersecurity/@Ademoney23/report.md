# Cyber Attack
---
Snowflake, the prominent cloud data platform, experienced a significant data breach in May 2024. The breach was orchestrated by hackers associated with the Scattered Spider group, who exploited compromised credentials of a Snowflake employee account.
This was a ransomeware attack. The attackers employed extortion tactics, demanding ransoms ranging from $300,000 to $5 million from affected companies to prevent the public release of the stolen data.

## Who were affected?

This unauthorised access led to the exfiltration of vast amounts of sensitive data. Billions of call records from AT&T and personal information from Ticketmaster and Santander Bank customers was stolen. 

## How could it have been prevented?

Proper training of employees on security practises. 

There should be proper access control within the organization.
